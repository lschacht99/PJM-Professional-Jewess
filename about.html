A note from the founder

Why I wanted to build this

My background is in mechanical engineering, and part of my career has been spent working with the U.S. Army. It's given me a window into a world of different jobs, experiences, and paths people take to get where they are.

But careers are about more than the job title. There are the questions that don't have an obvious place to go: Is this the right field for me? What is that career actually like? How do I get started? How do I balance the things that matter to me with the work I want to do?

I've also spent time bringing Jewish people together through community experiences. I love seeing what happens when people who might never have crossed paths get the chance to talk. Sometimes a connection is helpful in a very practical way. Sometimes it's simply reassuring to hear from someone who's been there.

That's what I want to bring to the professional world.

I don't think you need a huge network, a fancy title, or a perfectly mapped-out career to benefit from mentorship. Sometimes you just need the right person to answer one question honestly. And sometimes you're the person who can give someone else that answer.

So I'm starting small: bringing Jewish women from different fields and stages of life into the same network, listening to what they're looking for, and making thoughtful introductions where there's a good fit.

My hope is that this becomes a place where women are comfortable asking questions, sharing what they've learned, and helping each other find a way forward.

Leah
