ABOUT THE JEWISH MUTUAL

Curiosity deserves a chance.

We're building a Jewish network that helps young adults discover professional worlds through people who are willing to share their experience.

More than a conversation.

Imagine sitting with an architect over a set of plans. Asking an engineer how a prototype works. Trying a small project, or hearing what a healthcare career is really like.

Some experiences could happen over coffee or online. Others may involve workshops, demonstrations, mentorship or authorized workplace visits.

Explore example experiences

People first. Possibility next.

Our first phase focuses on mentorship and practical discovery opportunities for women, while welcoming Jewish young adults from different religious backgrounds and levels of observance.

Jewish identity, learning, community and real-world access are integral to the mission. Religious observance is not a disqualifier.

The network is being formed. Examples on this site are ideas, not confirmed programs or placements.

OUR BEGINNING

Built by someone who understands the gap.

The initiative was started by a mechanical engineer with U.S. Army civilian engineering and Jewish community-organizing experience. There is no government affiliation or endorsement.

The organization is still developing its programs and legal structure; tax-exempt status is not represented as approved.
