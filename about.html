ABOUT THE JEWISH MUTUAL

Curiosity deserves a chance.

We're creating ways for young Jewish women to discover professions, build skills, and meet people willing to share what they've learned.

Built around real experience.

It's hard to know whether a field is right for you until you've seen what the work involves. Our aim is to make that firsthand exploration more accessible, with Jewish observance accounted for from the beginning.

OUR BEGINNING

Founded by a mechanical engineer with U.S. Army civilian engineering experience and Jewish community organizing experience. This is an independent initiative; there is no government endorsement.

The Jewish Mutual is still developing its programs and legal structure. Tax-exempt status and fiscal sponsorship are not yet secured.
