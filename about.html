ABOUT THE JEWISH MUTUAL

Explore a career before you choose it.

We're building opportunities for young Jewish adults to experience different professions, learn from Jewish professionals, and find guidance as they go.

OUR PURPOSE

Make careers easier to understand.

It's hard to picture a profession when you've never had the chance to see it up close—or talk to someone who does it.

We want to change that through practical experiences and honest conversations. Career exploration comes first. Mentorship, professional connections, and continued guidance can follow.

WHAT THAT LOOKS LIKE

Different ways to find your direction.

01

Try something.

Join a practical workshop, project, or interactive online session.

02

See the work.

Learn through demonstrations or an approved workplace visit.

03

Talk it through.

Ask a professional questions, get advice, or build a mentoring relationship.

WHY A JEWISH NETWORK?

Your career and your Jewish life belong in the same conversation.

Choosing a career can raise questions that go beyond the work itself: Shabbat schedules, holidays, travel, family life, or finding your place in a profession.

Learning from Jewish professionals means being able to talk about those questions, too. Observance shouldn't exclude anyone from an opportunity. We're starting with young Jewish women in mind, while welcoming Jewish young adults from different backgrounds and levels of observance.

WHERE WE ARE NOW

Starting with what we can build.

The Jewish Mutual was started by a mechanical engineer with experience organizing Jewish community programs. Our initial experience ideas include a beginner-friendly engineering design challenge and opportunities to explore college, technology and interdisciplinary teamwork.

Other experiences can grow from participant interest, volunteer facilitators, and the professionals who choose to contribute—online and in person.

We'll begin by exploring demand for online activities and grow into other formats when they're practical.
