ABOUT THE JEWISH MUTUAL

Why we're building The Jewish Mutual.

Choosing a path is easier when you can actually see the work, try something yourself and hear from someone who's done it. We want to make those experiences possible for more Jewish young adults.

See it. Try it. Ask about it.

We're developing workshops, interactive online sessions, practical projects and experiences that give young people a closer look at different professions.

Our founder is developing an introductory engineering experience. Other sessions will grow out of the professionals and places willing to participate. Conversations and mentorship matter too, but we want to offer more than advice alone.

Explore the engineering experience in development

Learn from people who understand.

The experiences will be led or supported by Jewish professionals who understand the work and can also speak to the questions that come with Jewish life. Our initial focus is women-forward, while welcoming Jewish young adults across backgrounds and levels of observance.

Jewish identity, learning, community and real-world access are integral to the mission. Religious observance is not a disqualifier.

Programs are being developed. There are no confirmed public workshop dates, placements or guaranteed mentor matches.

HOW IT STARTED

A practical way to explore what's next.

The Jewish Mutual was started by a mechanical engineer with experience in Jewish community organizing. The idea brings those two worlds together: real professional insight and a community where people can learn from one another.

It's hard to picture a career from the outside. We want young Jewish adults to be able to ask honest questions, see the work up close, and talk to people who understand the role Jewish life can play in choosing a path.

Help shape what comes next.

Curious about a field? Tell us what you'd like to explore. Already work in one? You can share your experience, even if it's just one conversation.
