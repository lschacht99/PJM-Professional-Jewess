Questions and answers

Everything you need to know before applying.

This is a founding pilot, so we're being clear about what is available now and what we're building toward.

Who can apply?

Jewish women exploring a career, already established in a field, returning to work, changing direction, or willing to share their experience. We welcome a wide range of professional and creative paths.

Do I have to be Orthodox?

We want women to feel comfortable discussing Jewish life and their professional ambitions. You don't need to belong to a particular community or level of observance to express interest.

Can I both ask for help and offer guidance?

Yes. Choose “Both” on the application. Many women have questions about one part of their careers while being able to help others with something different.

How are introductions made?

The founding pilot uses human review. We look for relevant experience and needs, then reach out about potential matches when someone suitable may be available. There is no automatic matching engine or public directory.

When is the first matching round?

November 8, 2026 is the priority application date for the first round; matching review begins November 16. Applications remain open afterward and are saved to the same database. Later applicants may be included in the November round if matching continues or considered for future rounds. We will contact you if we identify a potential fit, but an introduction is not guaranteed.

Am I guaranteed a mentor or mentee?

No. The availability of a relevant match will depend on the people who apply and their capacity. We'll contact you if there's a promising next step.

How much time do I have to commit?

That depends on what works for both participants. Mentors can indicate whether they would prefer one conversation, occasional calls, or more ongoing support.

Does it cost anything to apply?

There is no payment required to submit an application. Any future paid offerings or fees would be explained before someone agrees to participate.

Who can see my application?

Applications are stored privately for organizer review. Personal details are not published in a directory. You will be contacted before an introduction is arranged. See our privacy page.

What about member accounts, calendars, and events?

These are potential future additions, not features of the current pilot. We're starting with applications and personal introductions.
