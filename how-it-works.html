What makes a strong fit

Specific questions. Relevant experience.

Someone considering a legal career may want a practicing attorney's perspective. A designer, athlete, or entrepreneur may want guidance from someone who has built a career in a similar way.

We aim to make personal, useful introductions — rather than promise automated or guaranteed matches.
