CHALLENGE 01 · SHABBOS, ANYWHERE

A familiar problem. A completely different way to solve it.

What if designing for people who step away from their phones each Shabbos led to a smarter solution for everyone?

Your challenge brief

The situation: A Jewish woman is visiting a city she doesn't know. She has to plan her arrival, find somewhere to eat, navigate on foot and prepare for different accessibility or dietary needs.

The challenge: Invent a practical system or product that makes the experience easier, more welcoming and less stressful. The design must work for users who aren't using their phones during Shabbos.

Which role would you take?

Bring a different professional perspective to the same problem. You don't need previous experience.

WHY THE JEWISH MUTUAL?

Professional possibilities, with Jewish life part of the picture.

Finding your direction should include the questions that matter in your life: Shabbos, school, family, internships and what it feels like to belong in a profession. We're starting with young Jewish women in mind, including women whose observance is often overlooked by other career programs.

We want to make room for serious career exploration, practical challenges and honest mentoring conversations—without assuming everyone has the same background or the same goals.
