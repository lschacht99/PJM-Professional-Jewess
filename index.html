The Impossible Sukkah.

Design a folding sukkah, test a model, and discover how a structural or mechanical engineer would approach the problem.

Unfold the challenge brief +

Your mission

Design a compact, reusable sukkah for a small city space. Sketch a folding frame, prototype one moving joint, and build a scale model you can test with a fan.

EngineeringWill it fold, stand, and survive the wind test?
Design & accessibilityCan people assemble it and enter comfortably?
BusinessHow much would it cost to make and sell?
Jewish knowledgeWhich sukkah requirements would the design need expert guidance on?

What you make: a scale model and a short design pitch. The professional connection we'd build: an engineer reviews the prototype, explains how real structures are tested, and answers your questions about the job.

WHY THE JEWISH MUTUAL?

Professional possibilities, with Jewish life part of the picture.

Finding your direction should include the questions that matter in your life: Shabbos, school, family, internships and what it feels like to belong in a profession. We're starting with young Jewish women in mind, including women whose observance is often overlooked by other career programs.

We want to make room for serious career exploration, practical challenges and honest mentoring conversations—without assuming everyone has the same background or the same goals.
