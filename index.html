WHY THE JEWISH MUTUAL?

Professional possibilities, with Jewish life part of the picture.

Finding your direction should include the questions that matter in your life: Shabbos, school, family, internships and what it feels like to belong in a profession. We're starting with young Jewish women in mind, including women whose observance is often overlooked by other career programs.

Career conversations can include questions about observance and everyday Jewish life, without assuming everyone has the same background.
