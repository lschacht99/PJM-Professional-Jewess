EXAMPLE IDEAS · NOT CONFIRMED EVENTS

A glimpse into someone's professional world.

These examples show the kinds of experiences we're hoping to create. They could start with a conversation in a café, a small demonstration, or an invitation into a workspace.

Tell us what interests you

01 · ARCHITECTURE

Walk through an architect's day.

Start with a floor plan or a model. Hear how a project goes from a sketch to a real building: the clients, decisions, constraints and creative problem-solving involved.

Could happen: at a café with drawings, via a screenshare, in a personal studio, or at an office with permission.

I'd like to explore architecture

02 · ENGINEERING

Take a prototype apart—on paper or in person.

An engineer could bring a safe model, explain how it was designed, discuss what failed at first and offer a small challenge for students to try.

Could happen: a community room, online demonstration, personal workshop, or workplace where authorized.

I'd like to explore engineering
03

03 · REAL ESTATE

How does a property deal actually work?

Review a fictional listing together. Learn how a property might be valued, renovated and marketed, and what negotiations look like behind the scenes.

Could happen: over coffee with a laptop, via a virtual walkthrough, or at an approved location.

I'd like to explore real estate

MORE POSSIBILITIES

Not everyone needs a workplace visit.

A healthcare professional could discuss training paths. A designer could review a portfolio. A business owner could show how they price products. A professional could lead a workshop, a small applied project, or a Q&A.

Some ideas are easy to host independently; others need supervision or workplace permission. Employer-created internships are a longer-term possibility, not an existing offer.

05 · HEALTHCARE

What does a healthcare career really involve?

Ask a professional about education, daily routines, teamwork and difficult decisions. Explore different roles through a guided conversation or an appropriate educational demonstration.

Could happen: online, in a community venue, or through an approved supervised visit. No patient access is implied.

I’m interested in healthcare

All examples are illustrative. No particular professional, employer, experience, internship or placement is guaranteed or available to book.
