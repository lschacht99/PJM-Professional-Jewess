FIRST EXPERIENCE IN DEVELOPMENT · FOUNDER-LED

REGISTER INTEREST · NOT YET BOOKABLE

Inside engineering: from problem to prototype.

Engineering is more than equations or designing something on a computer. It's about understanding a problem, trying ideas, seeing what fails and making it better.

We're developing a beginner-friendly experience with The Jewish Mutual's founder, a mechanical engineer. The idea is to explore a simple challenge, sketch possible solutions, test a small model and talk about what engineering work is really like.

The session could be in person or adapted for an interactive remote format. That decision, along with the date, capacity and location, is still being worked out.
