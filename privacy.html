THE JEWISH MUTUAL / PRIVACY

Privacy policy

When you get in touch or register your interest, you trust us with some personal information. This is what we collect, what we do with it, and how you can ask us to change or remove it.

Last updated: October 19, 2026

What you share with us

If you register as someone exploring a field, we ask for your name, email address and areas of interest. You can also tell us where you are based, what kinds of experiences interest you and what you hope to learn.

If you offer to mentor, host or collaborate, we ask for contact details and information about your work and what you might be able to offer. If you use the contact form, we receive your name, email address and message.

Optional answers are your choice. You do not need to tell us about your religious observance or personal circumstances to express interest. Please do not put passwords, financial details, government ID numbers or other sensitive documents into a form.

How we use your information

We use submissions to reply to you, understand which fields people want to explore, plan possible experiences and speak with professionals who may want to take part. If you register your interest, we may email you about relevant next steps as the network develops. You can ask us to stop contacting you.

Joining an interest list does not guarantee an introduction, a mentor, an internship or a place in a program.

Who sees it

Submissions are for the people managing The Jewish Mutual, not a public directory. We do not sell your personal information or publish your application. If there is a possible introduction to a mentor, employer or host, we will discuss it with you before sharing your contact details or identifiable application information with them.

Service providers may handle information as part of running the website and its forms. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Where it is stored

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Form submissions are sent to a Supabase database; they are not displayed on the public website. Some pages also use Google Fonts. Those services may process technical information such as your IP address and browser requests when you visit the site.

We use HTTPS to transmit form submissions and restrict access to submitted records to people authorized to manage them. As with any online service, we cannot promise that a system will never experience a security problem.

How long we keep it

We keep submissions while they are relevant to your inquiry or our work developing the network. We have not yet established a fixed automatic deletion schedule for early-stage registrations. If you no longer want us to hold your information, please ask us to remove it. Some limited records may need to be kept for legal or operational reasons.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you submitted, or tell us you no longer want follow-up emails. Use our contact form, choose “Access, correction, or deletion of my data,” and include the email address you originally used. We may need to confirm that the request is yours before making a change.

Links to other websites

We link to outside organizations, articles and resources. Once you leave our site, their privacy practices apply.

About this project

The Jewish Mutual is still being developed. We do not represent it as having independent tax-exempt recognition or a confirmed fiscal sponsor. As the project and its systems change, we will update this page.
