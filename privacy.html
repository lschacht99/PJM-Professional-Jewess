Privacy

Your information is for connection, not public display.

We collect only what you choose to submit as part of this early-stage professional network.

What we collect

Applications include your name, email, preferred contact method, optional location and professional background, plus what guidance you need or offer. If you select phone or WhatsApp contact, you provide that number. Contact inquiries include your name, email, and message.

How we use it

The organizer reviews applications to understand interest, consider introductions, and contact participants about potential next steps. Contact messages are stored so the organizer can review and reply separately.

Who can access it

Applications and inquiries are stored in a restricted database and are not available for public reading. They are intended for organizer review. We will reach out before sharing contact details as part of a proposed introduction.

Your choices

Applying is voluntary. If you'd like to ask about the information you submitted, request a correction, or request deletion, send us a message with enough information to locate your application.

Early-stage program

Professional Jewess is in a founding pilot. We don't currently provide applicant accounts or public member profiles. We may revise these practices as the program develops, and we'll update this page when they change.
