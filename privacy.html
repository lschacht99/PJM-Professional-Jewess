Privacy & transparency

Your curiosity is yours. Your information stays private.

Here is what happens when you contact us or tell us what you would like to explore.

What we collect

When you submit an interest or partnership form, we collect the contact details and interests you choose to share. We use this information to consider relevant professional experiences, respond to your questions, and explore possible collaborations.

How we store it

Form submissions are stored in a private database hosted by Supabase. The forms use a public submission key with insert-only database policies; public visitors are not given access to read application records. Authorized administrators may review applications. The website itself is published through GitHub Pages.

Who receives your information

We do not publish submitted applications. We do not sell your information. If an introduction or experience may be appropriate, we would contact you first and ask permission before sharing relevant details with a potential professional host.

Your choices

You do not need an account to explore the website or download free resources. Submitting an interest form is optional and does not guarantee a place in any program. You may ask us to correct or remove your application through the contact channel on the site once available.

About the organization

The Jewish Mutual is a working name for an emerging educational initiative. Its independent charitable or tax-exempt status has not been represented as approved. Any future donations or sponsorships will be handled under the applicable legal arrangements.

Images and examples

Some photographs are illustrative concept imagery. Example programs are clearly labeled and do not imply completed activities or confirmed corporate partnerships.

This notice describes the website's intended data flow as of October 2026. We will update it as operations and public contact details are finalized.
