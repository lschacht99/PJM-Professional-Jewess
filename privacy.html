PRIVACY & TRANSPARENCY

Your information should stay in your control.

Here is how we handle the information you choose to share while we build The Jewish Mutual.

What we collect

When you register an interest or offer to contribute, we ask for contact details, fields of interest and the experiences you would like to explore or help create. Contact inquiries may include information you choose to provide in a message.

Why we collect it

We use it to review interest, consider possible professional connections, discuss collaborations, and reply to questions or privacy requests. A submission does not guarantee matching, a visit or an opportunity.

Storage and access

Form submissions are stored in a Supabase database. The public forms have limited submission permissions; they are not meant to give public visitors access to other people's applications. The public site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Authorized administrators can review submitted information.

Sharing

We do not publish applications or sell submitted personal information. Before sharing your relevant information with a prospective professional host, we would contact you to discuss the proposed introduction and seek permission.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of an application or inquiry. Use the contact form, select “Access, correction, or deletion of my data,” and provide the email used in your submission. We will review requests and may need to verify identity before taking action.

Organizational status

The Jewish Mutual is an emerging initiative. Independent charitable or tax-exempt status and fiscal sponsorship are not represented as approved. No donation tax treatment is promised.

Illustrations and proposed experiences

The website uses illustrative artwork. Example sessions, workplace visits, projects and internship ideas are proposals, not currently bookable programs or confirmed placements.

Last reviewed October 2026. This notice will be updated as the organization and its procedures develop.
