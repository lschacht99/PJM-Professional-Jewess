Field guide / 01

Make a career conversation count.

01

Start with the real question.

What are you deciding? What don't you understand about the work? Share that in advance when possible.

02

Ask about ordinary days.

Which tasks fill most of the week? What surprised them? What is difficult or misunderstood about the profession?

03

Understand the entry path.

Which skills, prerequisites, credentials, or demonstrations of ability matter most?

04

Leave with a next action.

Identify one concrete way to test the field further. Thank the professional for their time.

Important: Religious practice varies, and institutional accommodation policies differ. These materials are educational templates, not individualized legal advice or guaranteed accommodation approvals. Adapt them to your circumstances and consult your institution or qualified advisor as needed.
