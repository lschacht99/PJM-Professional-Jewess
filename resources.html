A note on the dates

These examples use United States / Diaspora dates and observance patterns. Jewish dates begin at sunset; local times, Israeli practice, personal observance, university policies, and employer arrangements may differ. Adapt each request to the actual conflict, and discuss accommodations early. For official planning references see Hebcal’s 2027 calendar and EEOC workplace guidance.

These materials provide general information and sample wording, not individual legal advice or verification of religious practice.
